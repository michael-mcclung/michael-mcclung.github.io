About

Where technology and day-to-day operations meet.

I'm a software engineer turned field service operations leader, and I sit where technology and day-to-day operations meet.

At JPMorgan Chase, I spent three years building enterprise applications with Java 17, Spring Boot, Angular, and Oracle. I'm open to roles in field service and dispatch operations management, technical operations, FSM software implementation, business systems analysis, and software engineering, across Dallas–Fort Worth, remote, or hybrid.
